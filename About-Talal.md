## Rules for a Great About Profile (2026 best practices)

1. **Write in first person** — "I" builds authenticity; third person feels corporate.
2. **Lead with value, not your title** — skip "I am a software developer"; say what you do for people.
3. **Be specific** — replace adjectives ("hard-working") with proof (numbers, projects, outcomes).
4. **Be brief** — an elevator pitch; short paragraphs and bullet points beat walls of text.
5. **Show personality** — light wit works if it's genuinely you; never force humor.
6. **Include a CTA** — tell readers exactly what to do next (email, book a call, follow).
7. **Add contact + social links** — email, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio.
8. **Update regularly** — refresh every 3–6 months or after major milestones.
9. **Avoid buzzwords without substance** — "passionate," "guru," "ninja" need evidence.
10. **Be authentic** — write as yourself; the right audience will find you.
